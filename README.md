# Interactive VR

Virtual Reality (VR) is a way to step inside a computer-made world. When you wear a headset or use a phone in something like the cardboard, the screen fills your whole view, and when you move your head, the world moves too — just like real life. You can look up, down, behind you, and feel like you're inside a new place, even though you're standing in your room.

Interactive VR means you can do more than just look — you can actually interact with the world around you. You might look at something to make it move, press a virtual button, pick something up, or trigger sounds and animations just by where you look or how you move. Instead of just watching, you're part of the scene, and the scene responds to you.
